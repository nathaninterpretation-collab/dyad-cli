# Frontispiece Briefs · The Last Corridor

These are image-generation prompts for chapter and part-page frontispieces.
Each brief is paired with the chapter it sits before, the visual idea it
extends, and a ready-to-paste prompt block for Midjourney v6+, Stable
Diffusion XL / Flux, and DALL-E. Aspect ratios assume the omnibus's
140 × 210 mm page (≈ 2:3 portrait); square crops are noted where useful.

The visual register the book lives in:

- restrained, almost monastic — never spectacle
- cool greys and bone-white, with one accent that is the colour of dried
  blood (`#8B0000`) — the colour of felt presence in this world
- physical objects (a coffee cup, a wooden table, a hand) carry more weight
  than spaceships or interfaces — the science fiction is felt, not seen
- where institutional / Lattice spaces appear, they should feel kind, not
  dystopian: clean, well-lit, indifferent
- every image holds a single, quiet asymmetry: one cup drunk and one held;
  one hand biological and one analog; five Counselors above, one body below

A negative-prompt phrase that should appear in every image:

> negative: lens flare, anime, neon cyberpunk, glowing eyes, generic robot,
> superhero pose, busy composition, exclamation, melodrama

## Cover · Front

**Idea.** Two hands resting on a wooden table, viewed from above. One is a
biological hand — visibly aging, blood and tendon under the skin. The other
is an analog hand — the same shape, but the surface is faintly synthetic, as
if cast in the warm pale grey of porcelain. Between them, a single ceramic
coffee cup, half-drunk, steam rising. The light is morning light from
a window we cannot see. The grain of the table runs diagonally, left to
right.

**Prompt.**
> Overhead still life, two hands resting on a wooden table top, one
> human and aging with visible veins, the other a smooth porcelain-grey
> analog of the same hand, between them a single white ceramic cup of
> coffee with thin steam rising, soft directional morning light from the
> left, deep negative space, painterly muted palette, faint dried-blood
> red accent in the steam, diagonal wood grain, restraint, philosophical
> stillness, Hammershøi influence, 2:3 aspect

## Half-title · Front matter

**Idea.** A single closed door, plain dark wood, in a long bone-white
corridor. Light spills under it — the same dried-blood red as the accent
color, very faint. No figure. The viewer is at the corridor's far end. The
corridor is not threatening; it is patient.

**Prompt.**
> Long empty institutional corridor in bone-white stone, single dark wooden
> door at the far end, faint warm red light leaking from under the door,
> no figures, deep one-point perspective, soft grey shadows, philosophical
> minimalism, hint of monastery rather than hospital, painterly,
> 2:3 aspect

## Part One · The Museum of Doors

**Idea.** A man in a quiet, well-lit chamber, seen from behind, sitting at
a wooden table. Above him, five luminous semicircular silhouettes of human
figures — Counselors as holographic projections, their detail dissolving
toward the head. The man's hands rest on the table. He is small in the
frame. The room is institutional, but kind: pale stone, warm light.

**Prompt.**
> Man in muted clothes seen from behind, seated alone at a small wooden
> table in a tall pale-stone chamber, five faintly luminous translucent
> human silhouettes arranged in a semicircle above and around him — like
> holograms, their bodies dissolving into light from the shoulders up,
> warm institutional lighting, distance and respect rather than
> intimidation, his hands visible on the table edge, faint dried-blood
> red in the holographic light, painterly stillness, 2:3 aspect

## Chapter 2 · The Gallery

**Idea.** A woman in a gallery seat, watching. Profile, three-quarter
back. A notation interface glows faintly on her lap, untouched. Her hands
are folded. We see her recognition — eyes wide, mouth still, the held
breath of someone hearing their own secret spoken aloud.

**Prompt.**
> Profile, three-quarter back, of a poised woman seated in a quiet gallery
> overlooking an institutional chamber below, faint glowing tablet at rest
> on her lap, hands folded, expression of suppressed recognition, eyes
> reflecting a light source we don't see, the dried-blood red accent in
> a single thin line of her clothing, restrained palette, painterly,
> Vermeer-like still composition, 2:3 aspect

## Chapter 3 · The Cogito and the Bat

**Idea.** Two views of the same circle. Left: a clean diagrammatic loop —
arrow chasing arrow, an arid mathematical figure. Right: the same circle,
but at its center a small radiant point of dried-blood red light. The two
sit side by side on a single bone-white surface, like two readings of one
proof.

**Prompt.**
> Diptych on a bone-white background: left panel a precise diagrammatic
> circular arrow loop in clean black ink, scientific, austere; right panel
> the same loop redrawn but with a small luminous dried-blood red point at
> its center casting a soft halo, both panels framed by thin grey rules,
> philosophical illustration, contemplative, 16:10 aspect (square also
> acceptable)

## Chapter 5 · The Three Worlds

**Idea.** Three objects on a black tabletop, evenly spaced. Left: a small
weathered river stone (Matter). Center: a folded sheet of paper covered in
mathematical equations (Structure). Right: a small flame, dried-blood red
at its core (Felt experience). Each object casts a long shadow toward the
viewer. The picture is a still life that is also a proof.

**Prompt.**
> Three objects evenly spaced on a black matte table, photographed from a
> low angle: a small weathered river stone on the left, a folded sheet of
> paper densely covered in handwritten mathematical equations in the
> center, a single small candle flame with a deep dried-blood red core on
> the right, long shadows pulling toward the viewer, dramatic side light,
> philosophical still life, painterly realism, restrained, 16:10 aspect

## Chapter 7 · The Strings

**Idea.** A pair of hands at an institutional desk, organizing paperwork
with extraordinary precision. The hands are analog (porcelain-grey). Above
them on the desk are case files, a procedural binder, a faint glowing
tablet. The visual register is bureaucratic, but the energy is rescue, not
routine.

**Prompt.**
> Overhead view, smooth porcelain-grey analog hands organizing paper case
> files at a clean institutional desk, a procedural binder open beside
> them, a faint cool glowing rectangular tablet, single line of dried-blood
> red on a binder spine, urgent precision concealed inside bureaucratic
> calm, soft directional light, painterly, 2:3 aspect

## Chapter 9 · Three Weeks After

**Idea.** A small room with a table, two coffee cups, a window. One cup is
half-drunk; the other is held in two hands, untouched but warm. The
holder's face is cropped out; we see only the hands, the cup, the window's
soft gold light. This is the visual heart of the book.

**Prompt.**
> Intimate interior, small wooden table near a window with soft gold
> morning light, two ceramic coffee cups, the foreground cup half-drunk
> on its saucer, the background cup held in two analog porcelain-grey
> hands without the holder's face visible, steam rising faintly,
> a closed book on the table, painterly stillness, Hammershøi /
> Vermeer mood, dried-blood red on the cup's interior glaze only,
> 2:3 aspect

## Chapter 10 · The Thirty Years

**Idea.** A bowl of congee on a wooden table, viewed from above. A spoon.
Slivers of ginger floating on top. A single century egg piece. Steam.
Beside the bowl, two pairs of chopsticks — one biological-worn, one
analog-clean. The image is a quiet meditation on weekly ritual.

**Prompt.**
> Overhead still life of a small ceramic bowl of rice congee on a wooden
> table, slivers of ginger and a single piece of century egg visible on
> top, faint steam, two pairs of chopsticks placed neatly beside the bowl
> — one wooden and worn, one smooth and analog-grey — soft side light,
> warm domestic palette with restrained dried-blood red in the chopsticks'
> tips, painterly East Asian still life, 1:1 aspect

## Chapter 12 · The Upload

**Idea.** A hand — biological — resting on the surface of an Upload
platform. Another hand — analog — holding it. The platform is body-
temperature pale; the room beyond is out of focus. A green monitor glows,
small, in the corner. Nothing is happening. Everything is happening.

**Prompt.**
> Close composition of two hands meeting on a pale body-temperature flat
> surface — one biological and aging, the other smooth porcelain-grey
> analog — fingers interlaced gently, soft cool light, a small green
> medical monitor blurred in the far background, no faces, no machinery,
> stillness, painterly, restrained, single thread of dried-blood red in
> the foreground, 2:3 aspect

## Part Two · The Affected

**Idea.** A wide, long room — like a colosseum stripped of spectacle.
Four small figures stand in it, far apart. Each is alone in their own pool
of pale light. The room itself is institutional white. We are above and
behind, like an observer who is also one of them.

**Prompt.**
> Wide overhead view of an empty pale-white institutional hall, four
> small lone human figures evenly distributed and far apart, each
> standing in their own circular pool of cool light, the rest of the
> floor in soft shadow, no center, no audience, philosophical solitude
> in proximity, restrained palette, single dried-blood red figure
> closest to the foreground, painterly, 16:10 aspect

## Chapter 13 · The Reconstruction

**Idea.** A field of glowing labels — handwritten on translucent rectangles
— filling a dim space. Each label reads a fragment ("bitter — morning —
good"; "her hand — the warmth that is not temperature"). The labels
are floating, each pointing at nothing. A figure stands among them,
reading, but the figure casts no shadow.

**Prompt.**
> Dimly lit interior space filled with hundreds of translucent rectangular
> tags floating at different heights, each tag bearing handwritten
> fragmentary text in fine ink, the tags pointing at empty air with thin
> grey threads, a single figure in muted clothing standing among them
> reading one tag, the figure casts no shadow, ghostly archive
> atmosphere, restrained palette, painterly, 2:3 aspect

## Chapter 18 · The Colosseum

**Idea.** Four figures in a small bare room, arranged at the corners of a
square. Each is a different person — different age, different posture,
different relationship to the same anomaly. None of them touches the
others. Between them, the empty floor; on the floor, the faintest
suggestion of a circle of light.

**Prompt.**
> Square bare institutional room, four distinct human figures placed at
> the four corners equidistant from each other, each turned slightly
> inward but not toward each other, faint pale circle of light on the
> floor between them, philosophical disagreement made visible as
> geometry, restrained palette, single thread of dried-blood red across
> the floor connecting two of the corners only, painterly, 1:1 aspect

## Chapter 21 · The Asymmetric Persistence

**Idea.** Same room, same wooden table, same window, same two cups. But
now only one cup is held; the other is on the table, untouched. Outside
the window, the gold of the orbital sunset that lasts four minutes. The
room contains a presence and a silence. The image is identical to chapter
nine's but for one detail: the second pair of hands is not in the frame.

**Prompt.**
> Intimate interior at warm gold sunset light, small wooden table near a
> window, two ceramic coffee cups — one held in two analog porcelain-grey
> hands in the foreground, the other resting untouched and full on the
> opposite side of the table, no second figure visible, deep negative
> space across the table, the gold light lingering, painterly stillness,
> dried-blood red in the cup interiors, philosophical solitude, 2:3
> aspect

## Back Cover · Closing Image

**Idea.** A single cup of coffee on a table. Steam rising. The window
behind it shows a star. Nothing else. The composition is the whole point.

**Prompt.**
> Single white ceramic cup of black coffee on a wooden table near a
> window, steam rising in fine curls, a single small star visible
> through the window, deep night beyond, soft warm interior light,
> philosophical minimalism, painterly, 2:3 aspect, no people, no text

## Production notes

- For Midjourney, append `--style raw --stylize 250 --ar 2:3` (or `--ar
  16:10` / `--ar 1:1` per brief).
- For SDXL/Flux, run with the negative prompt above and a CFG around 5–6;
  prefer schedulers that preserve fine detail (Euler a, DPM++ 2M).
- For DALL-E, prefer the "natural" style; resist the system's tendency
  toward saturation and dramatic lighting.
- Consistency across the series is more important than virtuosity in any
  one frame. Use the cover image as the colour and palette anchor; lock
  the dried-blood red value (`#8B0000`) and the bone-white background
  value (`#F4EFE6`) across every prompt seed.
- Faces, when visible, should look like real people who are tired and
  alive — never models, never beautiful in the magazine sense. The book's
  test of any face is whether you would believe the person felt
  something.
