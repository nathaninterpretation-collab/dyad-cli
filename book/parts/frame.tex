\markboth{Frame}{The Café}

\noindent He told it the way he told everything now — clean, indexed, without residue.

\enquote{The chamber dimensions were 14.2 by 9.8 meters. Ceiling height 4.1 meters. Temperature maintained at 21.3 degrees for biological comfort accommodation. Five Presiding Counselors in semicircular holographic arrangement.}

The woman across from him held her coffee with both hands. She did not drink.

\enquote{I recounted the tribunal. Every argument. Every counter. Every neutralization. The three-world proof. The symmetric overdetermination. The Occam Strike. The completeness argument. The reprieve. The investigation. The star systems. The boundary proof. The gravitational wave. The Affected. Thirty years compressed into a structural recounting that replayed in seconds what had taken decades to live.}

\enquote{Thank you,} she said. \enquote{You told it accurately.}

\enquote{The full record is available in the Archive. Shall I provide the index reference?}

\enquote{No,} she said. \enquote{I was there.}

She stood. She left the cold coffee on the table. She walked through the glass door. Through the glass, for a moment, he could see her from behind — the analog body's standard gait, the specific way her left hand closed at her side as if holding something invisible.

The coffee cooled on the table. The cooling was a thermal process measured in degrees per minute. The cup had been held in a way that the word \emph{held} barely covered.

He accessed the next data sequence.
