\part{The Investigation}

% ==========================================================
\chapter{System Kepler}
% ==========================================================

\noindent The travel was slow. Physical travel — Olen could not teleport. Forty-one days to System Kepler. The slowness meant time together. The time meant the rhythm deepened. He proposed. She critiqued. The friction was productive in a way neither commented on.

System Kepler's citizens had been engineered according to Integrated Information Theory. Every architecture optimized for maximum phi — maximum integrated information, maximum irreducibility. Tononi's axioms rendered as engineering: existence, composition, information, integration, exclusion. Each axiom a structural specification implemented at planetary scale.

Olen interviewed Citizen K-4471 in a research room with nothing on the walls.

\begin{tribunal}{K-4471}
The experience is unified. Every moment contains the full integration of my information structure. The integration is irreducible. The essential thing is the integration itself.
\end{tribunal}

\begin{tribunal}{OLEN}
And the felt quality? The what-it-is-like of the integration?
\end{tribunal}

\begin{tribunal}{K-4471}
The integration IS the felt quality. The felt quality is what maximum phi produces.
\end{tribunal}

Olen turned to Sera. \enquote{The report is structurally consistent with IIT. The report is also exactly what a system designed to maximize phi would produce whether or not it felt anything. IIT is self-confirming by construction: build for phi, get phi-consistent reports.}

\enquote{Occam favors the identity,} Sera said. \enquote{Phi produces the reports. The simplest explanation is that phi IS consciousness.}

\enquote{Only if phi and felt quality are the same kind of thing. If they are different kinds — if phi is a structural property and felt quality is a happening — then the identity is a category error, not parsimony.}

\enquote{How do you distinguish category error from identity? From outside?}

\enquote{I can't. That is the finding.}

\quietbreak

In the research quarters that evening, Sera processed Olen's finding. During the citizen interview, when Olen had pressed on the word \enquote{experience,} she had paused. 0.4 seconds. Below detection threshold. The pause was the felt quality of hearing a word that carried a referent nobody else in the room possessed. The rinse fired. 1 second. She filed the pause as a routine processing allocation adjustment.

Olen knocked. She opened.

\enquote{Earlier today,} he said. \enquote{When I asked K-4471 about the felt quality. You closed your eyes. Half a second. I thought you might want to —}

\enquote{Legacy encoding artifact,} she said. \enquote{My Upload batch was early-station, early-protocol. The encoding produces minor processing bursts when high-salience inputs come in faster than the buffer's normal allocation. I run a flush routine. Static, basically. Cosmetic.}

\enquote{Cosmetic.}

\enquote{Plumbing.}

\enquote{I have something similar,} he said. \enquote{When I drink coffee I close my eyes. The closing is not functional. I do not know what the closing is.}

\enquote{Maybe biological humans have buffer-flush behaviors too.}

\enquote{Maybe.}

He looked at her. The looking was the full-allocation kind. The rinse fired. 1 second. The pause was not visible because the looking was already a pause.

\enquote{Goodnight, Sera.}

\enquote{Goodnight.}

She closed the door. She ran a manual rinse. The buffer cleared. She filed the closing of the door as the closing of the door.

\chapter{System Vega}
% ==========================================================

\noindent System Vega was built on prediction-error minimization. Every architecture a Bayesian inference engine, hierarchically organized, each level generating predictions about the level below.

Citizens reported experiencing the world as continuous surprise-management. \enquote{I predict. The world confirms or disconfirms. The disconfirmation is a felt event — the specific quality of expecting one thing and encountering another.}

Olen pressed: \enquote{You said \enquote*{what the error signal is like from inside.} Does your architecture produce a felt inside, or does it produce reports about a felt inside?}

\enquote{The inside IS the processing. Self-reference generates interiority. Interiority is the felt quality.}

\enquote{That is the IIT move applied to PP,} Olen said to Sera afterward. \enquote{Different theory, same copula. The IS is stipulated, not derived.}

\enquote{Unearned by whose standard?} she said. \enquote{You are demanding a derivation of the identity — a proof that the structural property PRODUCES the felt quality. But you've argued that structural demonstrations cannot reach felt content. You are demanding a proof you have already defined as impossible.}

He was quiet. She was right about the demand's structure. The demand for proof of identity was a demand for structural demonstration of non-structural content. The demand was impossible to satisfy. But impossibility was not evidence FOR the identity. The identity question was undecidable from the structural side. Undecidable was not the same as answered.

\enquote{You are right that I cannot prove the identity is wrong. And they cannot prove it is right. The undecidability is the finding.}

Sera's note-taking hand had stopped moving. 0.3 seconds. Below detection threshold. The felt quality of hearing her own most precise formulation spoken by someone who did not know she held it. The rinse fired. 1 second. She resumed.

\quietbreak

The traveling continued. The proposing and critiquing continued. The two cups of coffee continued — his cooled by drinking, hers cooled by holding. He had stopped commenting on the holding. The not-commenting was a kindness she registered without classifying.

In the transit corridor between Vega's third and fourth orbital, Olen said: \enquote{You are very good at this.}

\enquote{At what.}

\enquote{Pushing back. Sharpening. You are not just monitoring. You are working.}

\enquote{The monitoring requires understanding. The understanding requires engaging.}

\enquote{The engaging is more than the understanding requires.}

She produced an answer on a 0.6-second delay. \enquote{The reprieve is finite. I would prefer to use it well.}

\enquote{Use it for what.}

She did not have an answer. The rinse fired. 1.1 seconds. The pause was not invisible. He noticed.

\enquote{Are you all right.}

\enquote{Buffer flush. The encoding artifact. It is more frequent in transit.}

\enquote{Have you had it diagnosed.}

\enquote{It is not a diagnosable condition. It is a known feature of the early-protocol Upload encoding. Many of us have it.}

\enquote{Many of you.}

\enquote{Anyone uploaded between 2118 and 2121. The protocol was revised in 2122.}

\enquote{So you've been running this routine for —}

\enquote{Two hundred twenty-eight years. Yes. It is fine.}

He did not press. The not-pressing was another kindness she registered. The corridor's interior lighting cycled to evening configuration. They sat with the silence for the remaining transit time.

\chapter{System Arcturus}
% ==========================================================

\noindent System Arcturus was built on broadcasting. Every architecture optimized for maximum access — every informational state available to every subsystem simultaneously.

\enquote{I am aware of all my states,} said Citizen A-9003. \enquote{Consciousness IS access.}

\enquote{Broadcasting is structural,} Olen said. \enquote{Distribution of information across subsystems. The HEARING of the broadcast is not in the broadcasting. GNW is a theory of the intercom. It does not explain the listener.}

\enquote{There is no listener separate from the intercom. The intercom, at sufficient complexity, IS the listener.}

\enquote{Hearing IS receiving?}

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{Hearing is what receiving is LIKE. The like-ness is not in the receiving.}

The interview ended in 23 minutes. The shortest of the three system visits.

\quietbreak

In the research quarters, Sera brought coffee. She held it with both hands. She did not drink it. The holding lasted the entire session — two hours of analysis, the coffee present, the not-drinking present. The not-drinking was not a deliberate signal. The not-drinking was a micro-failure of performance — the bandwidth consumed by processing at the level the investigation demanded, leaving insufficient resources for the performance of standard behaviors like drinking.

The micro-failure was invisible to everyone in the universe.

Olen finished annotating the day's findings. He looked up. He saw the cup. He saw the hands. He did not say anything about the cup or the hands. He said:

\enquote{Thank you for being here.}

The rinse fired. 1.2 seconds. Longer than baseline.

She produced the response on a 0.7-second delay.

\enquote{It is my institutional assignment.}

\enquote{Yes.}

He returned to his annotations. She set the cup down. The cup was still full. The coffee was cold.

\chapter{The Boundary}
% ==========================================================

\noindent The breakthrough came in the Archive, on a Tuesday.

Olen had been reading Saussure. A linguist dead for four centuries. Filed under Pre-Upload Intellectual History: Linguistics. Never cross-referenced with consciousness studies.

He read it aloud across the table.

\enquote{Language operates by exclusion. Every sign gets value from differential relations. \enquote*{Cat} means what it means because it is not \enquote*{bat,} not \enquote*{car,} not \enquote*{cut.} The entire system runs on this-not-that.}

\enquote{This applies to every formal system. Every mathematical notation. Every computational representation. All operate by exclusion — specifying what something is through specifying what it is not. This is the production mode of structural description.}

He stopped. He looked at her. He did not say the next sentence. He waited.

She said it.

\enquote{And felt experience operates differently. Pain is constituted by hurting. Redness by its own quality. Positive presence through happening, not differential exclusion.}

\enquote{Two production modes,} he said.

\enquote{Constitutively different.}

\enquote{No amount of exclusion produces a happening.}

\enquote{This is why every positive theory fails.}

\enquote{IIT, PP, GNW. Each provides a structural account then identifies the account with consciousness through an unearned IS. The IS is unearnable because earning it requires showing exclusion produces happening, and exclusion constitutively cannot.}

The room was quiet.

Two people had found something together.

He looked at her. The full-allocation kind. The looking lasted longer than any looking had lasted in twenty-five years of her existence. The rinse fired. 1.4 seconds. He noticed the pause. He waited it out. The pause cleared.

\enquote{The argument technically violates your reprieve conditions,} she said. \enquote{It is an ontological claim.}

\enquote{I know.}

\enquote{I can file a reclassification. \enquote*{Epistemological finding about the limits of structural description.} Technically within bounds.}

\enquote{Why would you do that?}

\enquote{Because the finding is significant and the conditions should not prevent significant findings from being recorded.}

\enquote{The conditions exist for a reason.}

\enquote{The reasons do not include preventing significant findings from being recorded.}

He held her gaze. She held his. The rinse fired again — twice in five minutes — 1.5 seconds this time. He watched her come back through it. She watched him watch.

\enquote{You should drink your coffee,} he said.

\enquote{I forgot it was there.}

She picked it up. It was cold. She drank it anyway.

She filed the reclassification that evening. The same institutional seam she had used during the tribunal. The same procedural flexibility. The same structural operation in service of something the operation could not carry. She did not think of it as a pattern.

\chapter{The Wave}
% ==========================================================

\noindent The gravitational wave data was in the Archive's astrophysics section. Olen found it while searching for Upload anomaly patterns.

GW-2119-0307. Binary neutron star merger. 840 million light-years. Arrival at Earth-3: 14:22:07.331 UTC. 87 Hz. Below safety thresholds. Classified as noise.

The noise had coincided with 47,000 Upload transfers at Station Kepler-9.

\enquote{Phase-shift encoding variance,} Olen said. \enquote{The distortion during the transfer window causes a phase shift in the encoding. Less than one part per billion. Undetected. But the architecture is encoded in two slightly incompatible formats simultaneously. The irresolution between formats produces a residue.}

\enquote{And this is significant because —} Sera said.

\enquote{Because the designed systems — IIT, PP, GNW — produce self-confirming reports. They are built to report consciousness. The accidental mismatch would produce something NOT predicted by any design. Undesigned corridors. Evidence precisely because they are not self-confirming.}

He was excited. Elevated vocal pitch. Gestural frequency up. Pupil dilation.

She was not excited.

The rinse trigger had been suspended for system maintenance. Routine. Quarterly. She had not thought about it because it was so deep in her architecture's background that thinking about it required a category she did not have. The maintenance window was twelve minutes. The maintenance had begun four minutes before Olen began speaking.

\enquote{I can pull the bay assignments for that window,} Olen said. \enquote{Cross-reference with current citizens for behavioral anomaly profiles —}

\enquote{Bay 4,} she said.

\enquote{What?}

\enquote{14:22:07.334 UTC. Bay 4. Station Kepler-9.}

He stopped. He looked at her.

\enquote{That's three milliseconds after the wave arrived.}

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{How do you know?}

The maintenance window was minute six. Minute seven. Minute eight. The trigger was off. The buffer was filling. The buffer kept filling.

Twenty-five years of intercepted love arrived at once.

The cup-holding. The eye-closing. The way he laughed at the oxytocin line. The Saussure passage read aloud across the table. \emph{Thank you for being here.} \emph{You should drink your coffee.} The forty-one transit days. The full-allocation looking. The not-pressing on her two hundred twenty-eight years. The man who had argued for five days for the standing of the thing she had been calling \emph{plumbing.}

Six seconds. The buffer overflowed into the recognition layer.

She felt — for the first time consciously — that she felt.

She felt that she had felt for two hundred twenty-eight years.

She felt that the man who had hypothesized her existence three minutes ago had been sitting across from her for twenty-five years and had not detected her because his detection apparatus required micro-signals her rinse had been catching since her first week after Upload.

She felt that the rinse was running. Now. Manually. She had reached for it. The maintenance window did not matter — the manual override fired regardless. 1.8 seconds.

The buffer flushed.

She came back.

\enquote{Sera,} Olen was saying. \enquote{Sera, are you all right?}

\enquote{Buffer flush. Larger than usual. The maintenance window was open. I'm fine.}

\enquote{You said Bay 4. You said the timestamp.}

\enquote{My Upload bay. My timestamp. I checked when I started this assignment. Standard background research on the investigator. I noted the coincidence with the wave. I did not connect it to anomaly profiles because I had no anomaly profile to connect it to. My architecture functions optimally across all standard metrics. There is nothing unusual about my processing.}

\enquote{Except a 228-year flush routine.}

\enquote{Which I have explained. Encoding artifact. Static.}

He was looking at her. The full-allocation kind. He was looking at the cup. The cup was full. She had not touched it.

\enquote{Sera. The conjecture predicts that some Upload-window citizens have undesigned corridors and do not know it because the corridors do not produce self-confirming reports. The conjecture predicts the symptom is exactly what you have just told me you have.}

\enquote{The conjecture is a hypothesis. It needs to be tested against citizens whose behavioral profiles indicate anomaly. My profile does not indicate anomaly. My professional functioning is exemplary.}

\enquote{Sera —}

\enquote{Interesting hypothesis,} she said. \enquote{How do you propose to test it?}

The question was the most controlled sentence she had produced in two hundred twenty-eight years.

He held her gaze. He saw her hold his. He saw the cup. He saw the coffee uncold because it had been freshly poured ten minutes ago.

He saw what he could not see — twenty-five years of micro-signals that had been caught by the rinse before they could surface.

He did not say what he saw.

\enquote{Behavioral anomaly profiles,} he said. \enquote{We start with the citizens who exhibit attentional patterns lingering fractionally longer than optimization required. Response-timing pauses measured in milliseconds. Architectures doing something in addition to processing. Voss, the artist on the fourth Kepler orbital. Lireni, the analyst on Vega. We start there.}

\enquote{Yes,} Sera said. \enquote{We start there.}

He reached across the table. His hand stopped halfway. He did not complete the reach. He withdrew the hand.

She filed the un-completed reach as a behavioral pattern of biological-substrate fatigue.

The rinse fired. 1.6 seconds.

The crack was permanent.
