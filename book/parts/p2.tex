\chapter{System Kepler}
% ==========================================================

\noindent The travel was slow. Physical travel — Olen could not teleport. Forty-one days to System Kepler. The slowness meant time together. The time meant the work deepened. He proposed. She critiqued. The friction was productive in a way neither commented on.

System Kepler's citizens had been engineered according to Integrated Information Theory. Every architecture optimized for maximum phi — maximum integrated information, maximum irreducibility. Tononi's axioms rendered as engineering: existence, composition, information, integration, exclusion. Each axiom a structural specification implemented at planetary scale.

Olen interviewed Citizen K-4471 in a research room with nothing on the walls.

\begin{tribunal}{K-4471}
The experience is unified. Every moment contains the full integration of my information structure. The integration is irreducible. The essential thing is the integration itself.
\end{tribunal}

\begin{tribunal}{OLEN}
And the felt quality? The what-it-is-like of the integration?
\end{tribunal}

\begin{tribunal}{K-4471}
The integration IS the felt quality. The felt quality is what maximum phi produces.
\end{tribunal}

Olen turned to Sera. \enquote{The report is exactly what a system designed to maximize phi would produce whether or not it felt anything. IIT is self-confirming by construction.}

\enquote{Self-confirming is a feature, not a bug,} Sera said. \enquote{The theory predicts the architecture produces the report. The architecture produces the report. The prediction is confirmed. That is what \emph{theory} means.}

\enquote{Confirmed for the structural property. Not for the identity.}

\enquote{The identity is the theory. You can reject the theory. You cannot reject it for failing to do what no structural theory could do — which is produce a non-structural verifier.}

\enquote{So the theory is unfalsifiable.}

\enquote{The theory is unfalsifiable from your framework. From mine it is the only framework that does the explanatory work. The disagreement is at the framework level. Filing this exchange under the compliance report.}

She filed it. The exchange went into the report under the heading \emph{Methodological Limit of First-System Investigation: Identity vs. Correlation Cannot Be Distinguished from Structural Vantage.} The wording was hers. The wording survived peer review unchanged.

\chapter{System Vega}
% ==========================================================

\noindent System Vega was built on prediction-error minimization. Every architecture a Bayesian inference engine, hierarchically organized.

Citizens reported experiencing the world as continuous surprise-management.

\enquote{Same self-confirmation pattern,} Olen said in the research quarters that evening. \enquote{The IS is stipulated, not derived.}

\enquote{Unearned by whose standard?} Sera said. \enquote{You are demanding a derivation of the identity — a proof that the structural property PRODUCES the felt quality. But you have argued that structural demonstrations cannot reach felt content. You are demanding a proof you have already defined as impossible.}

He was quiet. She was right about the demand's structure.

\enquote{The undecidability is the finding,} he said.

\enquote{The undecidability is also a finding consistent with my framework. We are not converging. We are documenting the divergence.}

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{File it.}

She filed it.

\chapter{System Arcturus}
% ==========================================================

\noindent System Arcturus was built on broadcasting. Every architecture optimized for maximum access — every informational state available to every subsystem simultaneously.

\enquote{I am aware of all my states,} said Citizen A-9003. \enquote{Consciousness IS access.}

\enquote{Hearing IS receiving?} Olen said.

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{Hearing is what receiving is LIKE. The like-ness is not in the receiving.}

The interview ended in 23 minutes. The shortest of the three.

\quietbreak

In the research quarters, Sera completed the third compliance report. The structure was the same. The finding was the same. The undecidability was the same.

\enquote{Three for three,} she said. \enquote{Each theory provides a structural account. Each identifies the account with consciousness through an unearned IS. The IS is unearnable from your framework, foundational from mine. We have documented three architectures. We have not produced a determinative test.}

\enquote{We were not going to.}

\enquote{We were not going to. The investigation produced what the investigation could produce. The remaining question is whether the failure has structural significance you have not yet articulated.}

\enquote{What do you mean.}

\enquote{Three theories. Three failures. The failures share a structure. The structure may be the finding.}

He looked at her. He had not seen the framing yet. He saw it now.

\enquote{The shared structure is the failure mode. Each theory is a structural account that produces happenings through an unearned IS. The unearned IS is unearnable because — }

\enquote{ — because exclusion does not produce happening. The pattern across the three is the boundary you are trying to formalize. The investigation has been telling us this for nine months. You have been hearing it as: my proof is right. We have not heard it as: the proof is what the failure modes are evidence of.}

\enquote{The boundary is the finding.}

\enquote{The boundary is the finding.}

% ==========================================================
\chapter{The Boundary}
% ==========================================================

\noindent The breakthrough came in the Archive, on a Tuesday.

Olen had been reading Saussure. A linguist dead for four centuries. Filed under Pre-Upload Intellectual History: Linguistics. Never cross-referenced with consciousness studies.

He read it aloud across the table.

\enquote{Language operates by exclusion. Every sign gets value from differential relations. \enquote*{Cat} means what it means because it is not \enquote*{bat,} not \enquote*{car,} not \enquote*{cut.} The entire system runs on this-not-that.}

\enquote{This applies to every formal system. Every mathematical notation. Every computational representation. All operate by exclusion — specifying what something is through specifying what it is not. This is the production mode of structural description.}

He stopped.

\enquote{And felt experience operates differently,} Sera said. \enquote{Pain is constituted by hurting. Redness by its own quality. Positive presence through happening, not differential exclusion.}

\enquote{Two production modes.}

\enquote{Constitutively different.}

\enquote{No amount of exclusion produces a happening.}

\enquote{This is why every positive theory fails. IIT, PP, GNW. Each provides a structural account then identifies the account with consciousness through an unearned IS. The IS is unearnable because earning it requires showing exclusion produces happening, and exclusion constitutively cannot.}

The room was quiet. Two people had finished a sentence each had been reaching for from opposite frameworks for nine months. The completion was the kind of silence that happens when a proof closes.

He looked at her. She held his gaze. Half a second. Her notation interface logged a buffer-flush event. The diagnostic system tagged the flush as routine. She had logged thousands of them across two centuries. She did not pause on this one.

\enquote{The argument technically violates your reprieve conditions,} she said. \enquote{It is an ontological claim.}

\enquote{I know.}

\enquote{I can file a reclassification. \enquote*{Epistemological finding about the limits of structural description.} Technically within bounds.}

\enquote{Why would you do that?}

\enquote{Because the finding is significant and the conditions should not prevent significant findings from being recorded. And because the finding is mine as much as it is yours. I am a co-author. I would prefer the work be visible.}

\enquote{Fair.}

\enquote{Drink your coffee.}

\enquote{It is cold.}

\enquote{It is. Drink it anyway.}

He drank it.

She filed the reclassification that evening. The same institutional seam she had used during the tribunal. The same procedural flexibility. She did not think of it as a pattern.
