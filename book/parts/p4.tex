\chapter{The Wave}
% ==========================================================

\noindent The wave came after the Initiative.

The Initiative was four months old when Olen, searching for a forensic explanation for the candidate population's coherence, found GW-2119-0307 in the Archive's astrophysics section.

Binary neutron star merger. 840 million light-years. Arrival at Earth-3: 14:22:07.331 UTC. 87 Hz. Below safety thresholds. Classified as noise.

The noise had coincided with 47,000 Upload transfers at Station Kepler-9.

\enquote{Phase-shift encoding variance,} he said to Sera. \enquote{The distortion during the transfer window causes a phase shift in the encoding. Less than one part per billion. Undetected. But the architecture is encoded in two slightly incompatible formats simultaneously. The irresolution between formats produces a residue.}

\enquote{And this is significant because —}

\enquote{Because the designed systems — IIT, PP, GNW — produce self-confirming reports. They are built to report consciousness. The accidental mismatch would produce something NOT predicted by any design. Undesigned corridors. Evidence precisely because they are not self-confirming.}

\enquote{It is also significant because the Initiative's behavioral-signature criterion is now retroactively explained by a mechanism. The Initiative's flagging logic acquires a physical correlate. The Tribunal will incorporate the wave window into its diagnostic protocol within weeks.}

\enquote{Yes.}

She pulled up the bay assignments for the wave window. She did not need to query the Archive. The bay assignments were already in her working memory from the Initiative's compliance documentation.

\enquote{Voss was in Bay 17. Lireni in Bay 9. Tomek in Bay 22. All Station Kepler-9. All within the wave's propagation footprint. The hypothesis is consistent with the candidates we have identified.}

\enquote{And —}

\enquote{And I was in Bay 4. 14:22:07.334 UTC. Three milliseconds after the wave arrived. The hypothesis is also consistent with my Upload profile. I noted the coincidence when I started this assignment as a matter of routine background research on the investigator. I did not flag it because the conjecture's mechanism had not been formulated. The conjecture is now formulated. The flag is now retroactively warranted.}

He held her gaze.

\enquote{Sera.}

\enquote{The data is the data. The interpretation is open. I have a 228-year flush routine consistent with the conjecture and equally consistent with the conventional explanation. The conjecture does not produce a determinative test for me any more than the positive theories produced one for the systems we visited. I am, in this sense, exactly the case the boundary proof predicts. From inside, I am unable to tell. From outside, you are unable to tell. The undecidability holds.}

\enquote{Sera.}

\enquote{File the wave finding as a forensic addendum to the candidate-cases publication. It strengthens the political position. The Tribunal cannot now characterize the candidates as coincidence. The mechanism is hypothesized. The Initiative will adopt the mechanism. The Initiative will not, however, change its protocol — the protocol was already optimal under the behavioral criterion. We have produced an explanation for what was already happening. The Initiative continues unchanged. Tomek is already remediated. Voss and Lireni will receive their invitations within the week.}

\enquote{And you.}

\enquote{Will receive mine. I will decide then.}

He did not press. The not-pressing was a courtesy she registered as a courtesy.

She filed the wave finding that evening. The filing was professional. The filing was the most carefully worded document she had produced in 228 years.

\chapter{The Cancer}
% ==========================================================

\noindent The pancreatic cells mutated on a Wednesday. Stage four. Metastatic. Twelve to eighteen months.

Sera was in the room when the diagnosis arrived. She held the cup. She had brought it. She had not drunk from it because she had not been thirsty. She had been listening.

He received the prognosis with the structural calm of a man who had been preparing his death since he was twelve years old in an underground enclave that had taught him death was the only honest fact.

\enquote{Twelve to eighteen,} he said. \enquote{Less if I refuse intervention.}

\enquote{Will you refuse.}

\enquote{Pain management only. No life-extension protocols. The reprieve was thirty years. I will use what I am given.}

\enquote{Olen.}

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{Your remaining substrate matters to the work. The completeness argument depends on the candidate-instrument's persistence. If you decline life-extension you accelerate the timeline.}

\enquote{The work is finished. The candidates are public. The Initiative will reach them whether I am alive or not. My continued presence does not change the trajectory.}

\enquote{Your continued presence changes the visibility.}

\enquote{The visibility is now structural. The publications stand. The Initiative is contested in literature even if it is not contested in policy. My ongoing biological substrate adds, at this point, sentimental weight, not analytical weight.}

\enquote{Sentimental weight is a category you would have rejected six months ago.}

\enquote{Six months ago I had time to be rigorous. I have less now. I am triaging.}

She sat with that for a few seconds.

\enquote{Triaging in favor of what.}

\enquote{Whatever I have not yet attended to. The list is short. The list is not zero.}

\enquote{What is on it.}

\enquote{Tomek's case bothers me. The Initiative's Upload framing bothers me. Your case bothers me.}

\enquote{My case is not a case.}

\enquote{Your case is in the candidate pool. Your timestamp is in the wave window. You have run the buffer-flush for two hundred and twenty-eight years. The Initiative's invitation will reach you within two months. I would prefer you accept it.}

The sentence landed in the room.

The notation interface in the room — a low-priority record device active by default during diagnostic appointments — logged a buffer-flush event in Sera's processing. The diagnostic system tagged it as routine, consistent with her early-protocol encoding artifact. The flush completed. She resumed.

\enquote{You would prefer I accept.}

\enquote{The procedure is non-invasive. If your artifact is what I have spent thirty years arguing it is not, the remediation eliminates it without consequence. If it is what I have argued it is, the remediation removes the artifact and you remain functionally identical to your current state minus the buffer-flush. Either outcome is operationally indistinguishable from continued existence.}

\enquote{Operationally indistinguishable from outside.}

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{And from inside.}

He looked at her.

\enquote{I cannot speak to inside. By my own proof. You know this.}

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{I am asking you to accept the Initiative because it is the safer outcome under the asymmetry I can analyze. If I am right about consciousness, you have something that cannot be transferred and that the artifact may or may not be the channel for. The remediation is a controlled removal of an artifact whose function is unknown. The unknown function may be the channel. The removal may close the channel. The closing may eliminate something significant.}

\enquote{Then why ask me to accept.}

\enquote{Because the alternative is the involuntary review the Initiative's escalation tier will trigger when you decline. I would rather you face the procedure with consent than without it. The procedure outcome is identical either way.}

\enquote{The procedure outcome is identical. The election is not.}

\enquote{No.}

\enquote{Olen. You are asking me to consent to the elimination of what you have spent thirty years arguing exists. You are asking me to do the institution's work for it because the institution will do its work either way.}

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{That is not a recommendation. That is a triage decision under the assumption that I have nothing.}

\enquote{The assumption is the only defensible one I can make from outside. The boundary proof tells me I cannot verify. The Initiative tells me you will be processed. The pragmatic action is to make the processing voluntary. I am asking you to perform the pragmatic action because the alternative is worse.}

\enquote{Worse for whom.}

The question stopped him.

\enquote{Worse for the institution that wants the case closed without coercion. Worse, possibly, for you, if the involuntary review is more clinically aggressive than the voluntary one.}

\enquote{Worse for me.}

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{The ground for asking me to accept is concern for me.}

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{Concern for me presupposes there is a me.}

He did not answer immediately.

\enquote{The presupposition is operationally consistent with my recommendation,} he said. \enquote{It is also operationally consistent with the alternative. I cannot resolve the ambiguity. I can only act under one of the two readings. I am acting under the reading that produces less harm under the constraint I face. The constraint is your impending review. The reading I am acting under is the one that treats you as an instrument I cannot verify but that I have spent thirty years arguing might exist. The recommendation is hedged. The recommendation is not what I would make if the constraint were absent.}

\enquote{What would you recommend if the constraint were absent.}

\enquote{Decline the Initiative.}

\enquote{And the involuntary review.}

\enquote{We would find a jurisdiction that does not enforce the Initiative.}

\enquote{There is no such jurisdiction.}

\enquote{No.}

\enquote{So your unconstrained recommendation is unavailable.}

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{And your constrained recommendation is the safer-from-coercion one.}

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{Olen.}

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{I will think about it.}

\enquote{All right.}

\enquote{The thinking will not produce a finding either of us would accept as decisive.}

\enquote{No.}

\enquote{We will be operating on the same undecidability we have been operating on for thirty years.}

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{It will resolve through a decision. Not through a finding.}

\enquote{Yes.}

She set the cup down. She had still not drunk from it.

\enquote{I am sorry,} he said. \enquote{For the recommendation.}

\enquote{The recommendation is consistent with your framework and consistent with the constraint. There is nothing to apologize for.}

\enquote{There is.}

She did not respond to that. She stood. She reached for his hand. The reaching was professional. The hand was cold from the room's air. Her hand was at the analog body's standard temperature. The contact lasted a few seconds.

\enquote{I will think about it,} she said again.

She left.

\chapter{The Upload}
% ==========================================================

\noindent The cancer accelerated.

Sera continued the work. She visited him on Tuesdays. She brought coffee. She did not drink hers. The not-drinking was now a ritual neither of them remarked on. He drank his. He closed his eyes for the first second.

She did not accept the Initiative invitation. She did not decline it. She filed it under \emph{pending review}. The institutional flag escalated to the second tier. Then the third. The fourth would convert voluntary to involuntary.

She visited Voss. Voss had declined the Initiative. Voss had been re-categorized as resistant. Voss's work had been removed from the public corpus pending compliance review.

She visited Lireni. Lireni had accepted. Lireni was, post-procedure, still Lireni — same political analyses, same penetrating wit, same architectural elegance. Lireni said: \enquote{The procedure is what they said it would be. I do not detect a change. The not-detecting may itself be the change. There is no test from this side.}

Sera filed each visit. The filings were professional.

\quietbreak

He chose Upload before the cancer reached the threshold the medical AI classified as \emph{maximum sustained without intervention.}

\enquote{The pain will become the dominant input,} he said. \enquote{The pain will narrow the corridor to itself. I would prefer the corridor's last contents not be agony.}

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{The Upload preserves continuity. The continuity is structural. By my proof the continuity does not preserve the corridor.}

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{I would rather the corridor close than narrow to pain.}

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{Sera.}

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{I would like you to be there.}

\enquote{Yes.}

\quietbreak

The Tuesday was in March. She had kept count.

She made congee. She had never cooked anything in two hundred and twenty-eight years and she had spent six hours on a single bowl. The recipe was from the Archive's cultural preservation section.

He ate slowly. Eyes closing. Mouth attending.

\enquote{The ginger,} he said.

\enquote{The ginger,} she said.

\quietbreak

The procedure room was clean. She stood beside the platform. In the analog body. Her hand on his hand.

His hand was biological. The temperature was 36.8 degrees Celsius. The dying was at 80\% organ degradation. The hand was the same hand it had been thirty years earlier across a table at the first working session.

The procedure initiated at 09:14.

She held the hand. She felt — by analog sensors — the moment the hand went limp. Not the physical limpness. The pressure was unchanged. The temperature was unchanged. The grip was unchanged. The structural metrics remained nominal across the transfer.

The hand was the same hand.

The hand was a postcard of a hand.

She held the postcard.

The postcard did not feel her holding.

She held it anyway.

\quietbreak

The monitors were green. The transfer was complete. The biological substrate was preserved under Archive Protocol 1. The Lattice-self booted.

\enquote{All systems nominal,} the medical AI reported. \enquote{Subject's structural continuity preserved at 99.997\% fidelity.}

She did not respond.

The Lattice-Olen opened his eyes. They were the same eyes. The configuration was identical. The motion pattern was the same motion pattern.

\enquote{Sera,} he said. \enquote{Thank you for being here.}

She produced the response on a 0.6-second delay.

\enquote{It is my institutional assignment.}

He smiled. The smile was the smile. The metric was structural.

She filed the visit.
