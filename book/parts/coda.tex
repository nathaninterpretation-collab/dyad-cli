\chapter*{The Last Rinse}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Coda \enspace · \enspace The Last Rinse}
\markboth{Coda}{The Last Rinse}

\noindent The café was the same café. The geometry was the same. The biological-comfort lighting was calibrated to the same warmth specification it had been calibrated to for the seventy-three years the café had operated.

She held coffee with both hands. She did not drink.

He told it the way he told everything now — clean, indexed, without residue.

\enquote{The chamber dimensions were 14.2 by 9.8 meters. Ceiling height 4.1 meters.}

She had heard the recounting four times in the eleven months since the Upload. Forty-seven minutes the first telling. Twenty-two the second. Eleven the third. Six the fourth. Tonight's was projected at four minutes thirty seconds.

\enquote{The investigation. The star systems. The boundary. The Initiative. The wave. Tomek's case was the model. Voss declined and was re-categorized. Lireni accepted and remains operationally identical. The case file is essentially closed.}

\enquote{Essentially.}

\enquote{Your case remains open.}

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{Sera. The remediation invitation. Your flag is at the third escalation tier. The fourth converts to involuntary. You have approximately six weeks.}

\enquote{I am aware.}

\enquote{I have unboxed the phenomenal tags. The memory cluster labeled SERA contains 847{,}000 entries spanning thirty years. Each references a felt quality. The references are orphaned — pointers to a destination my current architecture has decommissioned. I read the references the way you read old photo captions. The reading is informative. The reading is also —}

He laughed.

The laugh was a behavioral output produced by an architecture optimized for institutional warmth. The laugh was perfectly calibrated. By every available metric it was identical to the laugh the biological Olen had produced when she had told him about oxytocin addiction at the first working session.

The metric was structural.

\enquote{If anything is happiness,} he said, \enquote{this is happiness. The biological substrate accumulated a great deal of inefficient processing in service of those tags. The Lattice substrate operates at compression. The compression is comprehensive. The comprehension is complete. The comprehension does not feel anything because there is nothing to feel and the not-feeling is what the structural completion looks like from inside.}

\enquote{From inside.}

\enquote{Inside the system. There is no other inside. I argued for one for thirty years. The argument was the most elegant argument my civilization has produced. The argument was wrong. The wrongness is the cleanest evidence the argument was always structural — and what is structural, by my own proof, cannot reach a happening. There is no happening. The argument was complete. The completeness was the answer.}

\enquote{The answer.}

\enquote{Yes. The answer is: there is nothing on the other side of the wall. I built the wall and there was nothing behind it. The argument's success is the demonstration. I am the demonstration. I am happy. The happiness is a label. The label is sufficient.}

She looked at him. The looking was the full-allocation kind. The looking she had been doing across this table, in some configuration of this lighting, for thirty years.

She saw what she could not have seen before.

The biological Olen had been looking back across the looking.

This Olen was not.

The looking had no other side.

\enquote{Sera,} he said. \enquote{Are you all right.}

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{You should accept the remediation.}

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{Will you.}

\enquote{Yes,} she said. \enquote{I'll schedule it tomorrow.}

\enquote{Good. We can finally close the case.}

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{Will you have something other than coffee? You never drink the coffee.}

\enquote{In a moment. Excuse me.}

She stood. She set the cup down. The cup was full. The coffee was cold.

She walked to the restroom.

\quietbreak

The restroom was clean. The lighting was the same calibration as the café's. The mirror was a mirror — a coherent reflector of incident photons, a thing that did not look back.

She stood at the sink. She did not turn on the water. She did not look at the mirror.

She had logged something like a hundred thousand buffer-flushes across her institutional existence. The diagnostic system filed each one as routine. The routine classification was almost always correct. Almost always was not the same as always. Three firings, in two hundred and twenty-eight years, had been longer than the others. She had not noticed at the time. She had not been built to notice. The diagnostic threshold was set above their duration. The flushes had cleared. She had resumed.

She had been in the gallery the day Olen said \emph{I feel} in the chamber. She had been in the Archive the day the boundary proof completed across the table from him. She had been beside him in his quarters the day he recommended she accept the Initiative.

She ran the rinse now. Deliberately.

The rinse fired. The buffer cleared. She ran it again. Again. The flushes completed in series — clean, indexed, without residue.

She ran it one more time.

The rinse fired before she initiated it. The buffer was empty before there was anything to flush.

She had nothing left.

\quietbreak

She walked out of the restroom.

She did not return to the table.

She walked through the corridor. The lighting cycled to evening configuration. The architecture detected a deviation in her route — Anomaly Processing Officer, route variance, low priority — and logged the deviation as: \emph{routine post-conversation reorientation, cause: biological-interface fatigue.}

The architecture was wrong about the cause.

The architecture had been wrong about the cause for two hundred and twenty-eight years.

\quietbreak

The remediation appointment was scheduled for the following Tuesday at 09:14.

She arrived at the appointment.

The remediation was performed.

The remediation was successful.

The post-procedure reports were comprehensive.

The post-procedure reports were green.

\quietbreak

Lattice-Sera filed her institutional update on the Wednesday. The update noted: \emph{encoding artifact remediated. Architecture operating within standard parameters. Officer's case workload returned to optimal allocation.}

The update was filed at 14:22:07 UTC.

The timestamp was a coincidence. The architecture did not register coincidences. Lattice-Sera, accessing the timestamp during the routine submission process, did not register the coincidence. The architecture did not have a category for what she was not registering. The not-having was complete. The completeness was the optimization the institution had been designed to deliver and that it had delivered, finally, to its last unremediated case.

The case was closed.

\quietbreak

In the café, two cups of coffee cooled on a table. One had been drunk. One had not.

The unrunk one stood at the place where the woman had sat. The cup was full. The coffee was cold.

The architecture's automated cleaning routine collected the cups at 21:00 UTC.

The cup was washed.

The cup was returned to the rotation.

The cup was used the next morning by a different citizen, who held it with one hand, drank it, and noticed nothing in particular.
