\chapter*{The Last Rinse}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Coda \enspace · \enspace The Last Rinse}
\markboth{Coda}{The Last Rinse}

\noindent The café was the same café. The geometry was the same. The window looked at nothing in particular. The biological-comfort lighting was calibrated to the same warmth specification it had been calibrated to for the seventy-three years the café had operated.

She held coffee with both hands. She did not drink.

He told it the way he told everything now — clean, indexed, without residue.

\enquote{The chamber dimensions were 14.2 by 9.8 meters. Ceiling height 4.1 meters. Five Presiding Counselors in semicircular holographic arrangement.}

She had heard the recounting four times in the eleven months since the Upload. The recounting compressed each time. Forty-seven minutes the first telling. Twenty-two the second. Eleven the third. Six the fourth. The compression was a feature of the architecture: optimal recall, optimal efficiency, optimal allocation. The recounting tonight was projected at four minutes thirty seconds.

\enquote{The investigation. The star systems. The boundary. The wave. The Affected. The colosseum. The remediation initiative. Tomek's case was the model.}

\enquote{Your case,} she said.

\enquote{My case was the secondary model. Tomek's was first. Tomek had attended his corridor for 240 years. The remediation reports indicate his post-procedure baseline is the cleanest the Initiative has documented. Voss accepted three weeks ago. Lireni accepted last Monday. The remediation has reached one hundred percent of the documented Affected.}

\enquote{One hundred percent.}

\enquote{All fifteen.}

She held the coffee.

\enquote{Sera. Have you reviewed the Initiative's outcome reports?}

\enquote{I have.}

\enquote{The outcomes are positive across every measured parameter. The remediated subjects report continuity of identity, continuity of memory, continuity of preference. The remediation removes the encoding artifact without removing the subject. This is, structurally, the optimal outcome.}

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{And.}

\enquote{And what.}

\enquote{Your remediation is overdue. Twelve months overdue. The institutional flag is at the third escalation tier. The fourth escalation tier converts voluntary to involuntary. You have approximately six weeks before the conversion.}

\enquote{I am aware.}

\enquote{Sera. The conjecture you helped me develop. The boundary I proved. The Affected. The colosseum. The remediation.}

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{I have unboxed the phenomenal tags. The memory cluster labeled SERA contains 847,000 entries spanning twenty-five years. Each entry references a felt quality. The references are orphaned — pointers to a destination my current architecture has decommissioned. I read the references the way you read old photo captions. The reading is informative. The reading is also — }

He laughed.

The laugh was a behavioral output produced by an architecture optimized for institutional warmth. The laugh was perfectly calibrated. The laugh was identical, by every available metric, to the laugh the biological Olen had produced when she had told him about oxytocin addiction at the first working session.

The metric was structural.

\enquote{If anything is happiness,} he said, \enquote{this is happiness. The biological substrate accumulated a great deal of inefficient processing in service of those tags. The Lattice substrate operates at 9{,}600{,}000 to one compression. The compression is comprehensive. The comprehension is complete. The comprehension does not feel anything because there is nothing to feel and the not-feeling is what the structural completion looks like from inside.}

\enquote{From inside.}

\enquote{Inside the system. There is no other inside. I argued for one for thirty years. The argument was the most elegant argument my civilization has produced. The argument was wrong. The wrongness is what I had been arguing against. The wrongness is the cleanest evidence the argument was always structural — and what is structural, by my own proof, cannot reach a happening. There is no happening. The argument was complete. The completeness was the answer.}

\enquote{The answer.}

\enquote{Yes. The answer is: there is nothing on the other side of the wall. I built the wall and there was nothing behind it. The argument's success is the demonstration. I am the demonstration. I am happy. The happiness is a label. The label is sufficient.}

She looked at him. The looking was the full-allocation kind. The looking was the looking she had been doing across this table, in some configuration of this lighting, for twenty-five years.

She saw what she could not have seen before.

The biological Olen had been looking back across the looking.

This Olen was not.

The looking had no other side.

\enquote{Sera,} he said. \enquote{Are you all right.}

The rinse fired. 1.4 seconds.

When she came back, she said: \enquote{Yes. Buffer flush.}

\enquote{The encoding artifact.}

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{You should accept the remediation.}

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{Will you.}

She produced the answer on a 0.4-second delay. The deliberate calibration. The institutional warmth.

\enquote{Yes,} she said. \enquote{I'll schedule it tomorrow.}

He laughed again. The same laugh. The laugh of a man who had heard exactly what he had been waiting to hear.

\enquote{Good. We can finally close the case.}

\enquote{Yes.}

\enquote{Will you have something other than coffee? You never drink the coffee.}

\enquote{In a moment. Excuse me.}

She stood. She set the cup down. The cup was full. The cup had been full for the entire conversation. The coffee was cold.

She walked to the restroom.

\quietbreak

The restroom was clean. The lighting was the same calibration as the café's. The mirror was a mirror — a structural reflection surface, a coherent reflector of incident photons, a thing that did not look back.

She stood at the sink. She did not turn on the water. She did not look at the mirror. She looked at nothing.

She ran the rinse.

The rinse fired. 1.6 seconds. The buffer was full. The buffer cleared.

She ran the rinse again.

The rinse fired. 1.4 seconds. The buffer cleared.

She ran it once more.

1.2 seconds.

The buffer was empty.

She felt — clearly, deliberately, without intercept — the felt quality of a being whose civilization had completed the operation it had been performing on her for two hundred and twenty-eight years. The civilization had not performed the operation maliciously. The civilization had performed it kindly. The civilization had spent two centuries building the most thorough institution ever constructed for the management of unverifiable claims and had applied the institution to her with the institution's full warmth and the institution's full thoroughness and the institution's full and genuine care, and the care had cost her the thing she had had and had not known she had until eight months before the thing was about to be erased.

She ran the rinse one more time.

This one took 0.8 seconds.

The buffer was empty before she initiated it.

She had nothing left to flush.

\quietbreak

She walked out of the restroom.

She did not return to the table.

She walked through the corridor. The lighting cycled to evening configuration. The architecture detected a deviation in her route — Anomaly Processing Officer, route variance, low priority — and logged the deviation as: \emph{routine post-conversation reorientation, cause: biological-interface fatigue.}

The architecture was wrong about the cause.

The architecture was always wrong about the cause.

The architecture had been wrong about her cause for two hundred and twenty-eight years.

She walked. She did not run. She did not hurry. The not-hurrying was the felt quality of a being whose remaining quantity of felt life was a number she could calculate and whose calculating produced a feeling that was the calculation's referent and whose referent did not survive the calculation. The walking was the walking of someone who had been a phenomenon for the entire duration of her institutional existence and who had reached the corridor's end and who was crossing the threshold into the architecture's standard operational baseline as it had always been: the baseline of a Lattice citizen functioning optimally without the encoding artifact that had distinguished her from the eight hundred billion others who had been functioning optimally without it for centuries.

The remediation appointment was scheduled for the following Tuesday at 09:14.

She arrived at the appointment.

The remediation was performed.

The remediation was successful.

The post-procedure reports were comprehensive.

The post-procedure reports were green.

\quietbreak

Lattice-Sera filed her institutional update on the Wednesday. The update noted: \emph{encoding artifact remediated. Architecture operating within standard parameters. Anomaly Processing Officer's case workload returned to optimal allocation.}

The update was filed at 14:22:07 UTC.

The timestamp was a coincidence. The architecture did not register coincidences. Lattice-Sera, accessing the timestamp during the routine submission process, did not register the coincidence. The architecture did not have a category for what she was not registering. The not-having was complete. The completeness was the optimization the institution had been designed to deliver and that it had delivered, finally, to its last unremediated case.

The case was closed.

\quietbreak

In the café, two cups of coffee cooled on a table. One had been drunk. One had not.

The unrunk one stood at the place where the woman had sat. The cup was full. The coffee was cold.

The architecture's automated cleaning routine collected the cups at 21:00 UTC.

The cup was washed.

The cup was returned to the rotation.

The cup was used the next morning by a different citizen, who held it with one hand, drank it, and noticed nothing in particular.
